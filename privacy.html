常頌 SEMPER 私隱政策

生效日期：2026年11月11日

常頌 SEMPER（「本應用程式」）是一個時辰頌禱應用程式，由 Gervais Baudry 神父開發及發佈。

我們不收集個人資料

本應用程式不要求登入，不設帳戶，不收集、儲存或分享任何個人資料，亦不使用廣告或分析追蹤工具。

儲存在你裝置上的資料

你的設定（例如禮儀日曆、字型、字體大小、外觀）及已下載的經文和朗讀錄音，只儲存在你的裝置上，不會傳送給我們。刪除本應用程式即會一併刪除這些資料。

網絡連線

本應用程式會從 semper.attende.app（由 GitHub Pages 提供）下載經文更新，並從 audio.attende.app（由 Cloudflare 提供）下載朗讀錄音。如同所有網站，這些服務供應商可能按其本身的私隱政策，短暫記錄技術資料（例如 IP 位址）以運作及保護其服務。我們不會使用這些資料來識別你的身份。

兒童

本應用程式並非以兒童為對象，亦不會在知情下收集兒童的任何資料。

政策更改

如本政策有所更改，會在此頁面公佈並更新生效日期。

SEMPER Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

常頌 SEMPER (“the app”) is a Liturgy of the Hours app developed and published by Fr. Gervais Baudry.

No personal data is collected

The app has no sign-in and no accounts. It does not collect, store or share any personal data, and it contains no advertising or analytics.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (calendar, font, text size, theme) and downloaded texts and read-aloud recordings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Network connections

The app downloads text updates from semper.attende.app (hosted by GitHub Pages) and read-aloud recordings from audio.attende.app (hosted by Cloudflare). Like any website, these providers may briefly log technical data such as IP addresses to run and protect their services, under their own privacy policies. We do not use such data to identify you.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
